Research

Integrated navigation is mostly a study of failure modes. GNSS drops out under a bridge, inertial solutions drift without bound, LiDAR needs structure, cameras need light, and radar sees through weather but returns a sparse, noisy cloud. My work is on combining them so the estimate holds where any one of them would not.

Radar-aided localization

Using mmWave radar as a navigation sensor rather than a perception one — ego-velocity from Doppler, registration against sparse returns, and what that buys you when the other sensors are blind.

Inertial characterization

Understanding what a given IMU actually does before trusting it in a filter: correlating raw output against a known reference to recover orientation, scale and error behaviour.

Rigs and synchronization

Multi-sensor fusion is only as good as its timestamps. Building the hardware side — microcontroller triggering, disciplined clocks, vehicle interfaces — so the data arriving at the estimator is worth estimating from.
